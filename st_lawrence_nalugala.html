Welcome to St. Lawrence

A Community of Learners & Leaders

Located along the serene Garuga Road in Nalugala, Entebbe — just 2km off the Kampala–Entebbe Highway — St. Lawrence High School offers quality O and A Level education in a peaceful, nature-rich environment near Lake Victoria. We nurture academic excellence alongside character, discipline, and life skills.

What We Offer

Academic Programmes

Our curriculum follows the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) standards, preparing students thoroughly for PLE, UCE and UACE examinations across all streams.

O Level

Ordinary Level (S.1 – S.4)

A comprehensive four-year programme laying the academic foundation across all core subjects leading to UCE.

Mathematics English Sciences Humanities Commerce
A Level

Advanced Level (S.5 – S.6)

Specialised two-year programme with subject combinations preparing students for UACE and university entry.

Sciences Arts Business Technology
Boarding

Boarding Programme

Full residential facilities providing a structured, focused environment for students from across Uganda and beyond.

Supervised Study 3 Meals Daily Security
